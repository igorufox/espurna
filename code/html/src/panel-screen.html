SCREEN

What the screen of the board shows: its lines, the Matter QR code, blanking (the panel: OLED, LCD or NEXTION)

Screen
The keys are the board's too: a button with the action terminal command screen.key prev / next / enter / back (the M5Stack: A, B, C). A blanked screen only wakes up on a key. With a configuration in the PAGES panel the screen shows its tile pages instead of the lines.
Lines
The label on the left, the source on the right. Sources: time, date, datetime, ip, ssid, rssi, host, uptime, heap, wifi, mqtt, relay:0, sensor:temperature (or the magnitude number, sensor:0), mqtt:<topic> or mqtt:<topic>#<json key>. A label without a source is a line of text. The lines that do not fit are the next pages.
s, how long a page stays when the lines take more than one
Blanking and QR code
s, 0 - never; a key or a button with the action display-on wakes it (the tile pages: their own screensaver)
