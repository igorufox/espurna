The small I2C screen of the board: what each line shows

Screen
Lines
The label on the left, the source on the right. Sources: time, date, datetime, ip, ssid, rssi, host, uptime, heap, wifi, mqtt, relay:0, sensor:temperature (or the magnitude number, sensor:0), mqtt:<topic> or mqtt:<topic>#<json key>. A label without a source is a line of text. The lines that do not fit are the next pages.
s, how long a page stays when the lines take more than one

0..255, -1 - as the panel starts

on the I2C bus, 0x3c or 0x3d
the I2C bus of the board (the i2c module), shared with the I2C sensors
the pin that holds the panel in reset (Heltec WiFi Kit 32: GPIO16), none if it has none
